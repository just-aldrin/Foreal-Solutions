DOST–BINHI partnership

What’s for real?

By Meryll Klaryze Polaron · October 28, 2025

It started with an idea.

An idea that entered competitions, went through different versions, feedback, and late nights. Somewhere along the way, it became more than just a project.

On October 28, 2025, that idea took another step forward as Foreal Solutions entered into a formal partnership with DOST and WVSU BINHI Technology Business Incubator.

The DOST–BINHI Partnership Contract Signing marked a new chapter for FOREAL Solutions—not just as a team that builds for competitions, but as a startup taking its ideas beyond the classroom and into the real world.

The beginning · DAP–NextGenPH Startup Challenge 2025

Saan aabot ang twenty pesos mo?

By Mikaela Angela Patopaten · September 9, 2025

Inspired by a conversation and a Cornetto ice cream, friends Meryll Klaryze A. Polaron, Mikaela Angela G. Patopaten and Florence Elaine G. Soleño began questioning the realities behind food distribution: why is it that food has become so expensive these days?

Joined by Zalve Mae A. Magsael and Aldrin E. Bansuelo, the team brought the idea to the DAP-NextGenPH Startup Challenge 2025, where it evolved into AgriChain: a decision support system using predictive analytics and machine learning to connect agricultural supply with demand.

What began as a simple curiosity turned into Foreal Solutions: a commitment to developing practical, sustainable technologies through the eyes, ears and experiences of the everyday Filipino.
